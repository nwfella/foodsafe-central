FoodSafe Central is an independent project — not affiliated with the FDA, USDA, or any government agency.

Sources: FDA openFDA food enforcement records and USDA FSIS recall notices & public health alerts (meat, poultry and processed egg products — the categories FDA does not cover). Every item links back to its official notice.

Important: recall databases are never complete, and a product with no matching recall here is not confirmed safe. Brand, lot and UPC details are parsed from official text — always check the linked official notice before acting. FSIS classifications are published inside each notice.

If you suspect food poisoning or an allergic reaction, contact a healthcare provider. Report a problem to the FDA at fda.gov/report-problem or to USDA FSIS at fsis.usda.gov.
